A limit is only as strong as the place it's checked.

Most launch and limit tools work in your theme. They hide the Add to cart button, grey out the quantity box or run a script on the cart page. That stops shoppers who use the product page. It doesn't stop anyone who reaches checkout another way, and on a busy release, plenty of people do.

LimitKit runs as a Shopify Function inside Shopify's own checkout, which every order passes through. A shopper who skips the product page still meets your rule before they can pay, with your wording. There's nothing to hide, no theme code to edit and no products to unpublish.

What it does

  • Drops

    Release products at an exact time

    • Schedule a release to the minute, or lock products now and open them by hand.
    • Products stay published, with photos and price, so they can be shared and found before launch.
    • The product page shows when it opens, in each shopper's own timezone, with a live countdown.
    • Badges on collection and search pages show which products open later.
    • Afterwards, see units sold, revenue, orders and sell-through for the drop.
  • Members first

    Early access for the customers you choose

    • Pick customer tags, such as vip. Logged-in customers with a matching tag can buy from the members time.
    • Everyone else waits for the public time, or never gets in on a members-only drop.
    • Use one set of tags for the store, or give each drop its own.
    • The app previews what guests, logged-in shoppers and members will see before you switch a drop on.
  • Purchase limits

    Cap how many each customer buys

    • Per order, for every shopper, logged in or not.
    • Per customer across all their orders. Shoppers log in to buy, and refunds and cancellations give the allowance back.
    • Over a period, such as per month, and shared across several products.
    • If two checkouts a few seconds apart both get past a per-customer limit, the later order is listed for you to review.
  • Waitlist

    Keep the demand you'd otherwise lose

    • While a product is locked, shoppers can leave their email on the product page.
    • See the list in the app and export it as CSV.
    • Send each sign-up to your own tools by webhook, or have LimitKit email everyone once when it goes on sale.
    • Every email has a one-click way off the list.

Shoppers never see LimitKit's name: messages use your wording and your theme's styling, and waitlist emails come from your shop.

Set up in a few minutes

  1. Install

    Install from the Shopify App Store. Checkout enforcement switches on by itself.

  2. Add the product page block

    One click from the app opens your theme editor with the block ready to add. Collection badges are one more click.

  3. Create a drop or a limit

    Choose the products and the times or quantities. Check the shopper preview, then switch it on.
