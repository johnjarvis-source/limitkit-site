How a drop works

From announcement to sell-out, in three steps

  1. Create the drop

    Choose the products and the opening time. Let members in first if you like. A preview shows what every kind of shopper will see.

  2. Build the buzz

    The product page shows when it opens, with a countdown, and shoppers can join the waitlist. Collection pages show a badge.

  3. Open on the minute

    At the opening time the product can be bought. Purchase limits still apply, so one customer can't take the lot.

Questions

Common questions about drops

Do I need to hide or unpublish products before the drop?

No. Products stay published so they can be shared and found before launch. LimitKit stops them being bought until the opening time.

What stops bots adding it to the cart early?

While a product is locked, Shopify refuses to add it to the cart, whether the request comes from your theme, a cart link or a script. Checkout checks again before payment.

Which timezone are opening times in?

You set times in your store's timezone. Shoppers see the opening time converted to their own.

Can members get in before everyone else?

Yes. Give a drop a members time and an everyone time, or make it members only. Members are recognised by customer tag once they log in.

Does it work with my theme?

The product page block and collection badges work with Online Store 2.0 themes and take on your theme's fonts and colours. The checkout rule works whatever your theme.
