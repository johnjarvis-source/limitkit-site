Common questions

Does it work with my theme?

Yes. The rules are checked in Shopify's checkout, which every theme uses. The product page message and countdown, and the collection badges, are theme blocks you add in the theme editor; they work with Online Store 2.0 themes.

What stops someone using a cart link or express checkout?

Every cart and checkout is checked against your drops and limits, whatever path the shopper took to get there: the theme's buttons, a direct cart link, express checkout or the Shop app.

Will my shoppers see LimitKit?

No. Messages use your wording and your theme's styling, and waitlist emails are sent in your shop's name with replies going to you.

How do members get early access?

Choose one or more customer tags, for the whole store or for one drop. Logged-in customers with a matching tag can buy from the members time; everyone else waits for the public time.

How do per-customer limits count?

Per-customer limits count across all of a customer's orders, ever, or over a period you choose. Shoppers log in to buy, and refunds and cancellations give the allowance back. Counting starts when you add the limit.

What happens if I uninstall?

Shopify removes the app's checkout rule straight away, so locked products can be bought again. If something must stay unavailable, set it to draft first. Your store's data is deleted 48 hours after you uninstall.

How does billing work?

There's a free plan, and paid plans are billed through your Shopify invoice with a 14-day free trial. You can change plans from the app's Plan page, and uninstalling ends any paid plan.

What customer data do you keep?

Only what's needed to count limits and report results: IDs, quantities, amounts and dates, plus waitlist emails that shoppers enter themselves. See the privacy policy.
