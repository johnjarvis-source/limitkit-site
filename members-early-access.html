How it works

Members time, everyone time, checked at checkout

A perk only works if non-members can't use it. Hiding the button doesn't stop a cart link or express checkout. LimitKit's check runs in Shopify's checkout, so early access stays early.

  • Uses your existing tags

    Any tag on the customer counts: from a subscription or membership app, a loyalty program, or added by hand.

  • Members time and everyone time

    Members can buy from one time and everyone else from a later one, or straight away.

  • Members-only releases

    Keep a release for members and never open it to everyone.

  • Guests asked to log in

    A member is recognised once they log in, so guests see a prompt to log in at checkout.

  • A way to join

    Add a link to your membership page, shown to non-members on the product page.

  • Preview before it's live

    See exactly what guests, logged-in shoppers and members will see, before you switch it on.

Questions

Common questions about members access

Where do the member tags come from?

From whatever already tags your customers: a subscription or membership app, a loyalty program, or tags you add in Shopify. You tell LimitKit which tags count as members.

Do members have to log in?

Yes. A member can only be recognised once they're logged in, so guests are asked to log in at checkout.

What do non-members see?

The product, when it opens to everyone, and your message, plus a link to join if you add one.

Can someone skip early access with a cart link?

No. The check happens in Shopify's checkout, whatever route the shopper took to get there.

Can each release have different members?

Yes, on the Pro plan each release can use its own tags. On Starter, one set of member tags applies to the whole store.
