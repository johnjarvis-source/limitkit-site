Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

LimitKit ("the app") is a Shopify app provided by John Jarvis, Tasmania, Australia. This policy explains what the app processes when a merchant installs it on their Shopify store, why, and how it is deleted.

What we collect from merchants

Store name, domain, timezone and currency; the settings, drops, limits and wording you create in the app; and the access tokens Shopify issues to the app so it can work on your store.

What we process about your customers

To enforce per-customer purchase limits we receive order events from Shopify and keep, for each order that contains a limited product: the customer ID, order ID and order number, product IDs, quantities and dates.

To report drop results we keep, for orders that contain products in a drop: product IDs, quantities, amounts and the customer ID.

We do not read customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses from Shopify.

Waitlist

When a shopper joins a waitlist on your store, we store the email address they enter, the product, the wording they agreed to, their language, the time, and their customer ID if they were logged in. We use it only to show the list to you, to send it to tools you connect by webhook, and, if you switch it on, to email them once when the product goes on sale. Every email has a link to leave the list, which deletes their entry.

Service providers

  • Shopify: the platform the app runs on.
  • Fly.io: hosting, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Supabase: the database, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Resend: sending waitlist emails (processed in Tokyo, Japan).
  • Google Workspace: our support email.

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while the app is installed. When Shopify asks us to erase a customer's data, we delete their purchase records and waitlist entries and remove their customer ID from drop results. When the app is uninstalled, we delete all store data 48 hours later, when Shopify sends the store erasure request. Records of delivered webhooks are deleted after 30 days.

No selling or advertising

We don't sell data or use it for advertising.

Your rights and contact

Merchants can ask what we hold about their store, or ask us to correct or delete it, at support@limitkit.com. Shoppers should contact the store they bought from first: we act on the merchant's instructions. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
