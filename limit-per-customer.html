Purchase limits

Limit 1 per customer on Shopify, and make it stick.

Most limit tools hide the quantity box or run a script on the cart page, and shoppers who skip the product page never meet them. LimitKit checks your limit inside Shopify's own cart and checkout, so every order has to pass it.

Why limits leak

A limit in the theme only stops people who use the theme.

Resellers and bots rarely use your product page. They use cart links, express checkout or a script that adds straight to the cart. A rule that lives in the theme never sees those orders.

What you can limit

Set the rule once. Checkout enforces it.

  • Per order

    For every shopper, logged in or not. Adding one too many is refused with your message.

  • Per customer, across orders

    Counts everything a customer has bought, not just this cart. Shoppers log in to buy.

  • Refunds give it back

    Refunded and cancelled items return to the customer's allowance automatically.

  • Over a time period

    Per month, for example, so regulars can come back for more later.

  • Shared across products

    Two colourways, one limit: count several products together.

  • Orders to review

    If two checkouts seconds apart both get past a per-customer limit, the later order is listed for you.

At checkout

Questions

Common questions about purchase limits

Do shoppers need an account?

Only for per-customer limits, so LimitKit knows who is buying. Per-order limits work for guests too. When a per-customer limit applies, guests are asked to log in at checkout.

Does it stop express checkout and the Shop app?

Yes. The limit is checked in Shopify's checkout, which Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and the Shop app all go through.

What about orders placed before I add a limit?

Counting starts when you add the limit. Earlier orders don't count towards it.

Will it change my theme?

No theme code is edited. An optional block can show the limit on the product page; the rule works without it.

What happens when a customer returns an item?

Refunds and cancellations give the allowance back, so they can buy again up to the limit.
